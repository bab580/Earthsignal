FIELD DATA STATUS

What this device measures

SoilSense reports the raw analog value, its calculated soil-moisture percentage and a dry / moist / wet status. The firmware has not been verified here; do not assume it measures air temperature, soil temperature, water level or weather forecast.

Weather forecasts and historical rainfall remain separate from field sensor readings. A regional forecast does not measure conditions at an individual farm.

Sensor readings are observations from the connected device. Weather and rainfall data are contextual, not a guaranteed forecast or an automatic irrigation instruction.

PUBLISHED SPACE-CROP EVIDENCE

What NASA learned from lettuce grown on the ISS

This is a review of published spaceflight research, not a second live experiment. The NASA record describes red romaine lettuce grown in the Veggie system aboard the International Space Station. Compare research questions and methods; the ISS conditions and measurements are not interchangeable with your Earth-grown crop.

Crop and flight

‘Outredgeous’ red romaine lettuce grew in Veggie on the ISS for 33 days during the VEG-01 technology-validation test.

What researchers examined

Returned samples were analyzed for microbial food safety and chemistry, including elements, antioxidants, anthocyanins and phenolics.

Practical lesson

NASA reported both insufficient and excess watering as challenges associated with stressed plant growth.
